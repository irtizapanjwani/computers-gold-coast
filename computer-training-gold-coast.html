Frequently Asked Questions

Computer Training Questions, Answered.

What is the best way to start learning computer basics at home?

The best way is through structured training sessions, like ours, which guide you through essential tasks like managing files, browsing the internet, and using common apps in a clear, step-by-step format.

Do you offer beginner computer courses for adults?

Yes. We provide beginner computer training for adults with no prior tech experience, delivered in a stress-free, encouraging way.

Is your training suitable for remote workers or freelancers?

Yes, our training is ideal for remote professionals needing to improve digital tools like Zoom, Google Workspace, Trello, or time management software.

How long does each session last?

Sessions are typically one hour but can be extended based on your needs. You can book one-off help or a series of lessons for progressive learning.

Can businesses book recurring group training?

Yes, we offer corporate training packages and recurring sessions for small businesses to onboard new staff or upskill teams in programs like Xero, Microsoft Teams, or Dropbox.

What makes your training different from free online tutorials?

Our training is personalised, live and interactive. Unlike passive YouTube videos or generic guides, we answer your questions in real time and adapt to how you learn best.
