Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered.

Can you create a full branding package with matching logo, cards and pamphlets?

Yes — we offer cohesive branding packages where your logo, business cards and pamphlets all follow the same visual language, keeping your brand consistent across every material.

Do you offer logo files suitable for signage or vehicle wraps?

Yes — we provide high-resolution vector files like AI, SVG and EPS, ideal for large-format printing, signage and vehicle branding.

What's the difference between a vector logo and a raster logo?

A vector logo (AI, SVG) can be resized without losing quality, great for print. A raster logo (PNG, JPG) is pixel-based and best for digital screens. We supply both.

Can you recreate or modernise an old logo I already have?

Yes — we offer logo refresh services to modernise outdated designs, enhance readability, and prepare your logo in scalable formats.

Do I get full commercial usage rights?

Yes. After final approval and payment, you get full commercial usage rights — you own your design and can use it anywhere you like.

What's the typical turnaround time for a business card and logo combo?

Most projects are delivered within 3–5 business days, with revisions finalised in 7–10 business days depending on feedback.
