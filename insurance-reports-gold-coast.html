What Is A Computer Insurance Report?

An Official Document, Written For Insurers.

A Computer Insurance Report is an official document prepared by a qualified technician outlining the condition and damage of your device (PC, laptop, Mac and more), the likely cause of damage — storm surge, liquid, drop, fire — repair or replacement recommendations, an estimated repair cost, and a formal statement supporting your claim.

Who Needs This Service?

Anyone Filing A Contents Claim.

Homeowners lodging claims under contents insurance, professionals using personal devices for work, students with damaged laptops covered by family insurance, and businesses filing claims for workstations or office laptops.

Common Scenarios

When You'd Need An Insurance Report.

  • Liquid spill — coffee or water damage to a laptop.
  • Power surge — a storm damages your PC.
  • Fire or smoke damage — to computer hardware.
  • Theft of devices — from home, car or workplace.
  • Accidental drops — screen cracks on laptops or MacBooks.

Having an accurate, detailed report speeds up your claim by providing clear proof of damage, ensuring it aligns with insurer policies, helping insurers verify repair or replacement costs, and reducing rejection risk from incomplete documentation.
