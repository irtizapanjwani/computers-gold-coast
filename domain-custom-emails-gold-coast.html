What Makes A Good Domain Name?

Short, Memorable, Relevant.

A good domain is short, memorable, relevant, and ideally includes keywords related to your services or location — something like plumbergoldcoast.com.au. To register a .com.au domain, you'll need a valid ABN or ACN proving you're an Australian entity. A .com.au domain reassures local customers you're a trusted Australian business, compared to a general .com.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered.

Why should I have a domain name for my business?

A domain name gives your business a professional online identity and makes it easier for customers to find you — it also builds trust and brand credibility.

Can I register a .com.au domain without an ABN?

No. To register a .com.au domain, you must provide a valid ABN or ACN proving you're an Australian entity.

What's a custom email and why is it better than Gmail or Hotmail?

A custom email (e.g. info@yourbusiness.com.au) makes your business look more professional, trustworthy and on-brand, unlike free email accounts.

Will I be able to use my new email on my phone and computer?

Yes. We set up your email account on multiple devices and email clients, including Outlook, Apple Mail and Gmail.

Can you migrate my existing Gmail inbox to a new custom email?

Yes — we perform email migration so your existing inbox history, folders and contacts are transferred to your new address.

Do you help with SPF, DKIM and DMARC records?

Yes. We configure these DNS records to ensure your emails are delivered securely and don't land in spam folders.
