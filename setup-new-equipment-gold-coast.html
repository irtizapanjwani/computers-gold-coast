Frequently Asked Questions

Equipment Setup Questions, Answered.

What kinds of devices do you set up?

We set up printers, scanners, webcams, smart gadgets, streaming devices like Apple TV and Chromecast, as well as accessories like external hard drives and smart speakers.

Can you help with devices I bought online?

Absolutely. Whether you purchased from Amazon, eBay or a local retailer, our team is familiar with a wide variety of brands and models.

Do I need to prepare anything beforehand?

Yes — please have the device unpacked and nearby with all included accessories, cables and instruction manuals.

How long does an onsite tech setup take?

Most appointments take between 30 and 90 minutes, depending on the number of gadgets and setup complexity.

Can you connect everything to my wireless network?

Yes. We specialise in secure WiFi setup and can connect all your gadgets to your existing network, or help configure a new one.

What if I already started the setup and got stuck?

No worries — our technicians can pick up where you left off, troubleshoot any issues, and complete the process properly.

Can you help with smart home automation routines?

Definitely. We can create voice-controlled routines, schedule device actions, and integrate them with platforms like Google Home or Alexa.

Do you offer packages for setting up multiple devices?

Yes, we offer bundled installation services that save you time and money when setting up multiple gadgets in one session.

Is this service available for businesses as well as homes?

Absolutely. We assist small businesses, home offices and professionals with setting up their workplace technology too.
