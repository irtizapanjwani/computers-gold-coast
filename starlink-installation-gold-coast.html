Is Starlink Right For You?

Starlink On The Gold Coast & Regional Queensland.

Starlink delivers high-speed, low-latency internet from a network of low Earth orbit satellites, with typical download speeds well above 100 Mbps and latency around 25 to 30 ms. For rural and semi-rural properties around the Gold Coast hinterland — Tamborine Mountain, Canungra and the Currumbin Valley — where NBN fixed line never arrived, it's often the fastest option available. It's also a strong backup connection for businesses that can't afford downtime.

As at mid-2026, Starlink residential plans in Australia start from around $75 per month, with hardware available as a free rental (you pay shipping) or an outright purchase. Roam plans suit caravans and 4WD setups. Pricing and promotions change regularly, so check starlink.com/au for current offers at your address. We install the dish and set up your network — you keep your Starlink account and billing directly with Starlink, so there's no middleman markup on your plan.

  • Sky Muster satellite — limited data and high latency.
  • NBN fixed wireless — congestion problems.
  • No NBN connection — nothing available at your address at all.
  • Can't afford downtime — running a business that needs a reliable backup connection.

If you have fibre to the premises, NBN is usually the better and cheaper option. Not sure which camp you're in? Get in touch and we'll give you honest advice for your address before you spend anything on hardware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Starlink Installation Questions, Answered.

Can I install Starlink myself?

Yes, the kit is designed for DIY. But a successful DIY install depends on having an easy mounting spot with a clear sky view and a simple cable path. Two-storey roofs, tile roofs, tricky cable runs and larger homes with WiFi coverage problems are where professional installation pays for itself.

How much does Starlink installation cost on the Gold Coast?

It depends on the mount type, roof height and access, cable run length, and whether you want extras like mesh WiFi or ethernet. Get in touch and we'll give you a straight quote over the phone. No hidden fees.

Where should the Starlink dish be mounted?

Wherever it gets a full, unobstructed view of the sky. That's usually the roof ridgeline or a pole clear of trees. We check the location with the Starlink app before drilling anything, because even partial obstruction from a branch causes regular dropouts.

How long does installation take?

Most standard installs take 1.5 to 3 hours, including mounting, cabling, router setup and speed testing. Complex jobs with long cable runs or mesh WiFi take longer.

Do I need an electrician to install Starlink?

No. Starlink plugs into a standard power point and the cabling is low voltage. If your preferred router location has no power point nearby, we can advise on options.

Does Starlink work in storms?

The dish is built for harsh weather and keeps working through rain and wind, though very heavy storms can cause brief slowdowns. Correct mounting matters here: a securely fixed, properly weatherproofed install handles Queensland summer storms without drama.

Is Starlink better than NBN?

If you have fibre to the premises, NBN is usually the better and cheaper option. If you're on Sky Muster satellite, fixed wireless with congestion problems, or no NBN at all, Starlink is typically a big upgrade in both speed and latency. We're happy to give honest advice for your address before you spend anything.

I already have Starlink but the WiFi is terrible. Can you help?

Yes, this is one of our most common jobs. We fix coverage with mesh systems, access points and hardwired connections, and we can reposition badly placed dishes.
