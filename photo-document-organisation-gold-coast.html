Frequently Asked Questions

Photo & Document Organisation Questions, Answered.

Can you digitise old VHS tapes and slides?

Yes — we convert VHS, Video8 and MiniDV home videos into digital files, and scan 35mm slides and photo prints into high-resolution images, so they're preserved before the physical media degrades any further.

How do you deal with duplicate photos?

We consolidate photos from all your devices and accounts, then identify and remove duplicate and near-duplicate images so what's left is a clean, organised library rather than thousands of repeated shots.

Will my photos be organised in a way I can actually find things?

Yes — we typically organise by date and event into a clear folder structure, so you can browse or search for a specific occasion rather than scrolling through an unsorted camera roll.

Do you back up the photos once they're organised?

We recommend it — an organised library is only truly safe once it's backed up. We can set up cloud backup, local NAS storage, or both, as part of the same job.
