How do I set up my new smartphone or tablet?

Setup includes powering on the device, connecting to networks, logging into your accounts, configuring settings, and installing essential apps.

Yes — data transfer includes contacts, apps, messages, photos and settings using secure, efficient methods tailored to your device.

Is remote setup available?

Yes. Setup can be completed remotely via secure software for clients across the Gold Coast and beyond.

Most standard setups take between 30 and 60 minutes. Data-heavy migrations may take longer.

Do you offer training for seniors or non-tech users?

Absolutely. Personalised guidance is provided with patience and plain-English instructions.

Is my personal data protected during the setup?

Yes. Security and privacy are top priorities — no data is viewed without permission, and sensitive info is never stored.
