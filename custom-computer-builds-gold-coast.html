Custom vs Prebuilt

What's Best For You?

Prebuilt PCs

  • Pros — faster availability, full-system warranties, plug-and-play simplicity.
  • Cons — limited airflow and upgrade options, bloatware, lower-tier components hidden behind brand names, and often patchy post-purchase support.

Frequently Asked Questions

Custom PC Build Questions, Answered.

What's the turnaround time for a custom PC build?

Most builds take 3-7 business days, depending on component availability and build complexity.

Can I choose specific brands for my components?

Yes — you can choose trusted brands like Intel, AMD, Corsair, ASUS, MSI and more for every component.

Do you offer water cooling?

Yes — choose between an AIO (closed-loop) or a fully custom water cooling system for maximum performance and style.

Can you build a compact PC for a small space?

Yes, we specialise in SFF (Small Form Factor) builds using Mini-ITX cases and compact, power-efficient parts.

Can you build a PC ready for VR gaming?

Absolutely — VR-ready builds are tested for compatibility with Oculus, HTC Vive and other platforms.

What support do you offer after the build?

We offer ongoing hardware support, diagnostics, and help with future upgrades or troubleshooting as needed.

Do you offer data migration from my old PC?

Yes — we securely transfer your files, preferences and software to your new system as part of the build.

What warranty comes with the build?

We include a warranty covering both parts and labour.

Do you provide consultations to help choose parts?

Yes — we offer one-on-one consultations to plan every part of your build around your goals and budget.
