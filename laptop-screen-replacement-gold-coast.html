Frequently Asked Questions

Laptop Screen Replacement Questions, Answered.

What does a typical cracked laptop screen fix cost?

For most 1920×1080 IPS panels (Acer Swift, ASUS VivoBook, Lenovo IdeaPad and similar) the all-inclusive price ranges $195–$265. That covers part, labour, pickup/return and a 12-month warranty.

Can you offer same-day screen repair?

If we have your panel in stock (common Dell XPS, HP Pavilion or MacBook Air models), we can often finish onsite within 2 hours of arrival. Less common models may need to be sourced first.

Do you replace the full MacBook Pro screen assembly or just the glass?

Current Retina models are one-piece assemblies — we fit genuine Apple parts. Typical cost is $599–$799 depending on size.

My screen shows no image but the backlight still glows — can you fix that?

Yes, this "no display but backlight works" symptom is usually a failed LCD or T-Con board. A panel replacement restores the picture.

Is touchscreen digitiser replacement included for 2-in-1 laptops?

Yes — Surface Laptop, HP Spectre x360 and Dell Inspiron touch assemblies are stocked, and the quoted price covers the digitiser and LCD together.

Can I upgrade to OLED from IPS on supported models?

In most cases yes — ask for an OLED screen replacement quote. Expect roughly +$140 on top of standard IPS pricing.

What is your dead-pixel policy?

Any stuck or bright pixel spotted within 14 days triggers a free swap under our dead-pixel replacement promise.

Will a screen swap void my manufacturer warranty?

No. We use brand-matched parts and document serial numbers, so your remaining manufacturer warranty stays intact.

Can you provide an insurance quote for laptop screen damage?

Sure — a written PDF quote with part number, labour and GST so you can lodge a claim with Suncorp, NRMA, RACQ or any other insurer. See our insurance reports service for full damage reports.

Is Afterpay or ZipPay available?

Both — choose pay-in-4 at checkout for repairs over $300, handy for MacBook and OLED jobs.

What if the screen fails again within 12 months?

Rare, but covered — we replace the panel and labour free, no shipping fees, no excuses.
