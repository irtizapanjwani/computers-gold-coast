Common Signs You Need A Hinge Repair

A Faulty Hinge Rarely Fixes Itself.

Ignoring a hinge issue can lead to further damage to your screen, keyboard or internal components — and a bigger repair bill. Here's what to watch for.

↔️

Loose Or Broken Hinge

The screen is wobbly or won't stay in place at the angle you set it.

🔒

Screen Won't Open Or Close Properly

Stiff or unresponsive hinges can lead to cracks or breakage over time.

📐

Screen Separating From The Base

A visible gap between screen and keyboard means the hinge is failing.

🔊

Grinding Or Clicking Noises

Strange sounds when opening or closing usually mean internal hinge damage.

💥

Cracks Around The Hinge Area

Visible physical damage near the hinge is a clear sign of wear and tear.

⚠️

Left Untreated?

Ignoring hinge issues risks permanent screen damage, higher repair costs, and potential data loss if the laptop stops functioning.
