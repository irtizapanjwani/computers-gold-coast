Frequently Asked Questions

Data Recovery Questions, Answered.

How much does data recovery cost?

Pricing depends on the type of data loss (accidental deletion, hardware failure, ransomware), the device type (HDD, SSD, USB, RAID, cloud storage), and the severity of damage (logical vs. physical failure). Get in touch for a straight quote.

What should I do if I accidentally deleted my files?

Stop using the device immediately to avoid overwriting the lost data, don't attempt DIY recovery since incorrect attempts can make files unrecoverable, and call us as soon as possible for professional recovery assistance.

Can you recover files from a completely dead hard drive?

Often, yes — we have specialised recovery tools and techniques to retrieve data from dead or unresponsive drives, though results depend on the extent and type of damage.

Do you recover lost emails and cloud files?

Yes. We restore deleted emails and fix missing files in Google Drive, OneDrive, Outlook and other cloud services.

How long does data recovery take?

Simple file recovery is usually 1 to 2 hours. Hard drive recovery typically takes 24 to 72 hours. Complex cases involving RAID, SSDs or ransomware vary based on severity.
