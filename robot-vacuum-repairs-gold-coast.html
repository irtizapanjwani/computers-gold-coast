Common Robot Vacuum Faults

Recognise Any Of These?

Robot vacuums are small computers with motors and sensors attached — most faults come down to a handful of common causes.

🔌

Won't Charge Or Dock

Dirty charging contacts, a worn dock sensor, or a failing charging circuit are the usual culprits.

🔋

Battery Won't Hold Charge

Like any rechargeable battery, robot vacuum packs degrade over time and eventually need replacing.

🧭

Sensor & Mapping Faults

Bumping into things it used to avoid, getting lost, or refusing to map a room properly.

⚙️

Brush & Wheel Motor Issues

Tangled hair and debris are the most common cause of brush motor strain and eventual failure.

💨

Suction Loss

Clogged filters, blocked airways or a failing suction motor all reduce pickup performance.

📶

App & WiFi Connectivity

Won't connect, won't stay connected, or app scheduling stops working correctly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Robot Vacuum Repair Questions, Answered.

How much does a robot vacuum repair cost?

It depends entirely on the fault and the model. We diagnose first and give you an honest, upfront quote before any repair work goes ahead — no surprises.

Is it worth repairing an old robot vacuum, or should I just replace it?

Often it's worth repairing — many faults are a worn battery, a dirty sensor, or a clogged motor rather than something requiring a full replacement. We'll give you an honest opinion once we've had a look, including if replacement genuinely makes more sense.

Do you repair all robot vacuum brands?

We work with all the major brands sold in Australia. If you're not sure whether we can help with yours, give us a call with the model and we'll let you know.

Can you fix a robot vacuum that won't connect to WiFi or the app?

Often, yes — connectivity issues are usually a software or firmware problem rather than a hardware fault, and are generally straightforward to diagnose.
