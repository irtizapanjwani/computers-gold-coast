Frequently Asked Questions

ISP & NBN Plan Questions, Answered.

Is your comparison truly independent of all ISPs?

Yes. We accept zero commissions. Our only fee is the flat optimisation charge you pay, which is usually offset by the first month's savings.

Will I experience downtime during an NBN churn?

Most transfers complete in under 30 minutes. We schedule out-of-hours cut-overs for mission-critical offices.

Do you supply or configure new modems?

Yes — BYO-friendly Asus, TP-Link, or ISP-supplied hardware. We pre-flash the latest firmware and harden your WiFi passwords.

Am I eligible for a free fibre-to-the-premises (FTTP) upgrade?

We check your address against the latest NBN Technology Choice FTTP list. If you're on qualifying FTTN speeds and order 100 Mbps or above, you may upgrade at no cost.

I'm renting — can I still change NBN plans?

Yes, provided no new cabling is required. We supply a modem that plugs into the existing socket and get landlord approval if extra hardware mounts are needed.

What's the cheapest NBN plan for seniors?

We source discounted lower-speed "senior value" tiers plus any available concession-card rebates — typical savings run around $15 to $25 per month depending on your current plan.

How do you protect my privacy when reviewing my bill?

Invoices are stored in an encrypted vault, deleted after 30 days, and never shared with third parties.
