DC Jack & Motherboard Repairs

Can't Charge Unless You Wiggle The Cable?

That's usually an ageing DC jack — we repair it properly rather than just patching it. Dropped liquid on your laptop, or had it plugged in during a storm? The motherboard controls almost all of your laptop's hardware, and a repair is usually far cheaper than a full replacement.

For Home & Business

We Fix Common Laptop Issues For Businesses And Consumers Alike.

Whether your laptop needs to send emails, edit video, or just get you through the work day, these issues can bring you to a halt.

  • Dead laptop — no power at all, even when plugged in.
  • DVD drive not working correctly — or a disc that's stuck inside.
  • Faulty power socket / DC jack — charging only works at certain angles.
  • Broken, faulty or full hard drives — including data recovery where possible.
  • Faulty AC power adapter — or a battery that's failing entirely.
  • Programs fail to load — or the whole system freezes randomly.
