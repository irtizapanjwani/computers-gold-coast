Frequently Asked Questions

Cryptocurrency Onboarding Questions, Answered.

How much money do I really need to buy my first crypto?

You can start from as little as $20 AUD on exchanges like CoinSpot or Swyftx. A small test buy lets you practise deposits, withdrawals and wallet transfers before committing larger sums.

Which exchange is easiest for absolute beginners in Australia?

Both Swyftx and CoinSpot offer PayID deposits, a simple dashboard and AUSTRAC registration. We walk you through KYC, 2-factor authentication and your first purchase.

Do I need a hardware wallet immediately?

If you're holding less than $500, a reputable software wallet is fine. Above that, a Ledger Nano X or Trezor Model T cold wallet is strongly recommended for long-term security.

What's the safest way to back up my seed phrase?

Write the 12/24 words on paper, then consider engraving them onto a metal backup plate to survive fire or flood. Store it in a home safe or bank deposit box — never photograph or email the words.

What's the difference between cold storage and a hot wallet?

Hot wallets stay online and are convenient for daily use but vulnerable to hacks. Cold storage — a disconnected hardware wallet — keeps keys offline, ideal for long-term holdings.

Can you recover a lost seed phrase?

Sadly, no — seed equals keys equals coins. Prevention is everything, which is why our session includes multiple backup methods.

Do you store a copy of my keys or seed phrase?

Never. Keys stay client-side only. We simply guide you through creation and backup.
