Frequently Asked Questions

Troubleshooting Questions, Answered.

Why is my computer running slow?

Your computer may be slow due to too many startup programs, an old or failing hard drive (HDD vs. SSD), lack of RAM, malware or viruses, or outdated software that needs updating.

How much does troubleshooting & repair cost?

Pricing depends on whether it's a software issue vs. a hardware repair, the complexity of the problem, and whether replacement parts are needed. Get in touch for a straight quote.

Can you fix WiFi & internet connection issues?

Yes. We troubleshoot slow internet speeds, dropped connections and router issues, plus NBN, Telstra, Optus and TPG internet problems.

What should I do if my computer keeps crashing?

Stop using it immediately to prevent further damage, take note of the error messages and symptoms, and call us for professional troubleshooting.

Do you offer remote computer repairs?

Absolutely. If your issue is software-related, we can connect remotely to diagnose and fix the problem without a visit.
