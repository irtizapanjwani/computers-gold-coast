Frequently Asked Questions

Data Backup & Transfer Questions, Answered.

How do I back up my computer files properly?

We recommend a 3-2-1 backup strategy: 3 copies of your data (the original plus two backups), 2 different storage types (such as an external hard drive and cloud storage), and 1 offsite backup to protect against physical damage like fire or theft.

What's the safest way to transfer data to a new PC?

Secure options include a direct PC-to-PC transfer, an external hard drive backup and restore, or cloud migration using Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. We pick whichever suits the amount and type of data you're moving.

How do I fix OneDrive sync issues?

Common fixes include checking your internet connection and restarting OneDrive, making sure files are actually within the OneDrive folder, and clearing the cache before re-syncing with cloud storage. If it's still stuck, we can troubleshoot it remotely.

How much does professional data transfer cost?

It depends on the amount of data, the type of transfer (PC-to-PC, cloud, or external drive), and whether it's onsite or remote. Get in touch and we'll give you a straight quote over the phone.

Can I schedule automatic backups on my PC?

Yes. We configure Windows Backup, Mac Time Machine, and cloud sync services for hands-free, ongoing data protection.
