The Best Break-In Is The One That Never Happens.

Most burglars want an easy, quiet target. A visible alarm and a loud siren take your home off that list, because the risk of being seen and heard isn't worth it. That's the real value of an alarm: it stops trouble before it starts, and if someone does try, it makes enough noise to send them running and let you know straight away.

On larger and rural-style blocks around the hinterland, the siren matters even more, because a deterrent needs to be heard across a bigger property.

Frequently Asked Questions

Alarm System Questions, Answered.

Do I have to pay for monthly monitoring?

No. The systems I fit let you self-monitor straight from the app, so you get the alerts yourself with no ongoing fee. If you'd prefer a monitoring service to call out a patrol or notify contacts, that can be added, but it's an option rather than a requirement. You're not locked into a contract to use your own alarm.

Wireless or hard-wired, which is better?

For most homes, a modern wireless system is the practical choice. It installs cleanly without cutting into walls, runs up to two years on a battery, and is easy to expand. Hard-wired suits some new builds and larger commercial jobs. I'll recommend what fits your property rather than pushing one type.

Can my alarm work with my security cameras?

Yes, and it's a great combination. When a sensor trips, your CCTV can start recording and you can pull up the live view to see what actually happened, instead of guessing whether it's an intruder or a possum. I set both up to work together as one system.

Can you fix or take over my existing alarm?

Often, yes. If your panel is beeping, sensors keep dropping out, or you've moved into a home with an alarm nobody has the code for, I can reprogram, repair or replace what's there. If it's too old to be worth saving, I'll tell you honestly and quote a straight replacement.

Are you licensed to install alarms?

Yes. I hold a security installer licence (4481646), so alarm work is done properly and to the standard required. Combined with my cabling licence, that means the alarm, the cameras and the wiring behind them are all handled by the one licensed installer.
