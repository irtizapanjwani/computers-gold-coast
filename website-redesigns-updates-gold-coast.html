Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered.

How do I know if my website needs a redesign?

If your site is outdated, loads slowly, isn't mobile-friendly, or isn't generating leads, it's time to upgrade. We assess your website and recommend the right solution.

Can you redesign my WordPress site without losing my content?

Yes. We preserve your existing blog posts, service pages and contact forms while upgrading the design, theme and layout for better performance.

What platforms do you work with besides WordPress?

We redesign websites from Wix, Squarespace, Joomla, Weebly and others by rebuilding them on a faster, scalable platform like WordPress or a lightweight HTML template.

Can I keep my existing domain and email addresses?

Yes. Your domain name and email setup won't change — we'll update the hosting or connect the redesigned website to your current domain.

What happens to my SEO during a website update?

We maintain or improve your current SEO settings. That includes redirects, meta tags, page structure, and keeping your indexed content intact.

Is my website backed up during the redesign process?

Always. We take full backups before and after the redesign to protect your existing site and provide a fallback if needed.
