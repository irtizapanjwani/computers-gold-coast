Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered.

What makes onsite SEO different from traditional SEO services?

Onsite SEO involves a face-to-face consultation where we physically visit your business location to assess your website, implement improvements, and provide real-time strategy and support — a more personal, hands-on approach than remote SEO handled by an offshore team.

Can onsite SEO help my business appear in local map listings?

Yes. Optimising for the local 3-pack and Google Maps visibility is a core part of our strategy, including NAP consistency, business directory citations, and Google Business Profile optimisation.

How does onsite SEO improve my website's mobile performance?

We test your site on real mobile devices, perform mobile speed diagnostics, and fix issues with responsive design, improving both user experience and rankings on mobile-first indexing.

Do you set up SEO plugins during the visit, like Yoast or Rank Math?

Yes — we configure and optimise SEO plugins in real time, ensuring correct settings for sitemap generation, robots.txt, and on-page SEO scoring.

What if my website was built on a DIY builder like Wix or Squarespace?

We work with all platforms, including drag-and-drop website builders, and optimise them within platform limitations while offering advice for scaling SEO as your business grows.

How do you track and report SEO performance after the visit?

We install and configure Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and Bing Webmaster Tools to give you ongoing insight into traffic, rankings, and click-through rates.
