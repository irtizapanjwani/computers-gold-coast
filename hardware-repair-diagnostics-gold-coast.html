Software Troubleshooting

System Instability & Sluggish Performance.

Usually caused by software conflicts, OS errors or corrupted system files. We fix Windows boot errors, macOS kernel panics, corrupted registries and Blue Screen of Death crashes using SFC, DISM, Clean Boot and Safe Mode diagnostics — without unnecessary reinstalls or data loss.

Network & Connectivity

Slow Or Dropping Internet Connection?

We solve WiFi that won't connect or drops frequently, LAN port and router compatibility issues, NBN modem faults, and driver conflicts after OS updates — optimising your router configuration and re-establishing a stable, high-speed connection.

Virus & Malware Removal

Suspicious Pop-Ups Or Ransomware Alerts?

We scan and eliminate viruses, trojans, spyware, rootkits and keyloggers, install and configure antivirus software, remove browser hijackers, and restore corrupted system files — ensuring your system is clean and protected against reinfection.

PC Performance Optimisation

Computers Slow Down Over Time.

We uninstall bloatware, clean up startup processes, defragment hard drives, optimise SSDs, and upgrade RAM or storage — see our hardware installs & upgrades page for details. Older systems can often feel brand new again.
