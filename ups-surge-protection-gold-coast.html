Why It Matters

What Is A UPS & Why Do You Need One?

A UPS system ensures that when the power goes out, your devices stay on. It also protects sensitive equipment from power surges and spikes (common in Queensland summer storms), voltage fluctuations that damage electronics, blackouts and brownouts that cause downtime, and data loss and equipment damage. For homes and small businesses, one short outage could mean lost work, lost sales, or costly repairs.

  • WiFi & NBN Drops — modem, router, and VoIP phone shutting down mid-call.
  • Small Retail Shops — EFTPOS & POS systems crashing during sales.
  • Smart Home Devices — security cameras & alarms going offline in a blackout.

Frequently Asked Questions

UPS & Power Protection Questions, Answered.

What is a UPS and why do I need one?

A UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) provides backup power during outages and protects your devices from surges, spikes, and brownouts. It ensures your computer, modem, or business equipment keeps running long enough to save work or shut down safely.

What size UPS do I need for my home or business?

It depends on how many devices you need to protect and how long you want them to stay powered. For homes, a smaller unit (600VA–1200VA) is often enough, while small businesses may need 1500VA–3000VA units or higher.

How long will a UPS keep my devices running during a power outage?

Most UPS systems provide 5–30 minutes of power, depending on the load. Larger models with extended batteries can provide hours of backup.

Can a UPS protect against lightning strikes?

A UPS includes surge protection, but for strong lightning activity, it should be paired with a dedicated surge protector at the switchboard for full protection.

Will a UPS save me from losing unsaved work?

Yes. During a blackout, the UPS gives you time to save documents, finish work, and safely shut down devices.

Can you install UPS systems for both homes and small offices?

Yes — we provide tailored installation for home setups (PCs, TVs, modems, NAS drives) and small businesses (POS systems, servers, phone systems, networking gear).

Do I need a separate UPS for each device?

Not necessarily. One UPS can protect multiple devices, as long as the total power usage doesn't exceed the UPS's capacity.

How often should UPS batteries be replaced?

UPS batteries usually last 3–5 years. We can supply, replace, and safely recycle old batteries when they reach the end of life.

What's the difference between line-interactive and online UPS systems?

Line-interactive UPS is affordable and great for home and small office use. Online UPS provides continuous, clean power with no switching delay, ideal for sensitive equipment and small businesses.

Does a UPS save on electricity bills?

Not directly. UPS devices use a small amount of power themselves. Their benefit is in protecting devices from costly damage and preventing downtime.

Can a UPS protect my internet connection?

Yes. By connecting your modem/router to a UPS, you'll still have internet during a blackout (if your provider supports it).

Will my UPS beep during a power outage?

Most UPS units make an audible beep when running on battery to alert you. This can usually be silenced via software.

How much does a UPS installation cost?

Prices vary depending on the size and model of UPS needed, plus installation requirements. Home installations can start around $200–$400, while small business solutions may range higher. Get in touch for a straight quote over the phone.

Can you integrate UPS systems with my existing setup?

Yes — we can neatly integrate UPS units into your computer desk, server cabinet, or office networking rack.

Do I need a licensed electrician to install a UPS?

For standard plug-in UPS systems, no. But for larger hardwired UPS systems or switchboard surge protection, a licensed electrician is required — and we can organise this for you.

Can a UPS run my whole house or office?

No — UPS units are designed for essential equipment, not full power backup. For whole-building coverage, a generator or solar battery system would be required.

What brands of UPS do you install?

We work with trusted brands such as APC, CyberPower, Eaton, Vertiv, and Schneider Electric, depending on your needs and budget.

Will a UPS protect my devices from brownouts and voltage dips?

Yes — UPS systems regulate voltage to ensure your devices receive stable, clean power.

Can I monitor my UPS remotely?

Many modern UPS systems include software or apps that let you track battery health, runtime, and alerts directly from your computer or phone.

Because we provide tailored solutions for homes & small businesses, supply and install quality UPS units, offer local support across the Gold Coast, and handle battery replacements & ongoing maintenance.
