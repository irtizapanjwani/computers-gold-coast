Why It Happens

Locked Out Isn't Always Your Fault.

Accounts get locked for all sorts of reasons — a forgotten password, a phone upgrade that lost your authenticator codes, an old recovery email you no longer use, or someone else guessing or phishing their way in. Each platform has its own official recovery process, and it isn't always obvious which steps actually work.

How We Help

We Walk The Process With You.

We work through each platform's official recovery and verification steps with you, help you prove ownership where required, and once you're back in, we help you re-secure the account with a strong password and two-factor authentication so it's harder for it to happen again.

After You're Back In

Locking It Down So It Doesn't Happen Again.

  • Strong, unique password — no more reused or guessable logins.
  • Two-factor authentication — set up properly, with backup codes stored safely.
  • Recovery details updated — a current phone number and email you'll still have access to next time.
  • Suspicious activity check — reviewing connected apps, active sessions and recent changes for anything you didn't authorise.
