Frequently Asked Questions

Preventative Maintenance Questions, Answered.

How is preventative maintenance different from a one-off repair?

Preventative maintenance is a scheduled tune-up — dust clean, patch roll-up, backup test — aimed at avoiding failures before they happen. A one-off repair fixes something already broken, often at a higher cost and longer downtime.

How often should I get a tune-up?

Quarterly is ideal for business or heavy-use home PCs — every three months we patch software, refresh malware protection, clean internals and verify backups, keeping performance consistent year-round. Lighter home use can often stretch to twice a year.

Will a tune-up actually make my computer faster?

A registry clean alone rarely makes a big difference on its own. We combine safe registry pruning with startup-item auditing, drive health checks and dust/thermal cleaning for a noticeable, lasting speed boost.

Do you offer remote maintenance?

Yes — software-only tasks like patches, malware sweeps and driver updates can be handled over secure remote support. Hardware cleaning and thermal paste work still requires an onsite visit.

Do you verify that my backups actually work?

Yes — we run a real restore test rather than just checking that a backup job exists, so you know your files will actually come back if you ever need them.

Do I get anything in writing after the visit?

Yes — every session ends with a health report covering what was done, key health metrics, upgrade advice, and a recommended date for your next service.
