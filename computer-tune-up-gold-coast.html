Why Your Computer Needs A Tune-Up

A Computer Is Like A Car — It Needs Regular Maintenance.

Over time, unnecessary files, outdated software and security threats slow down your system. Common signs it's time for a tune-up: slow performance, frequent freezing or crashing, overheating and loud fan noise, too many startup programs, and pop-ups or strange behaviour that could mean malware or bloatware.

Frequently Asked Questions

Computer Tune-Up Questions, Answered.

How do I speed up my slow computer?

A computer tune-up removes unnecessary files, optimises software, and improves overall performance.

What does a computer tune-up include?

It includes removing junk files, updating software, scanning for malware, and optimising system settings.

Can I clean up my PC without losing files?

Yes. Our clean-up service ensures no personal files are deleted — only unnecessary system clutter is removed.

How often should I get a computer tune-up?

At least once a year for general maintenance, or every 6 months if you use your computer heavily.

What's the difference between a tune-up and a full reset?

A tune-up improves performance without deleting your files, while a full reset restores factory settings.

Do you offer onsite and remote computer cleaning?

Yes — we provide both onsite service and remote tune-ups for convenience.

How long does a computer clean-up take?

Most tune-ups take 1 to 2 hours, depending on the system's condition.

Can a tune-up remove viruses?

Yes — we scan and remove malware, adware and spyware that could be slowing down your system.

Will a tune-up make my PC faster?

Absolutely. A tune-up removes unnecessary clutter and optimises system settings to improve speed.
