Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions, Answered.

How is this different from voicemail?

Unlike voicemail that just records messages, the AI receptionist has actual conversations with callers. It asks questions, gathers specific details about their needs, and keeps customers engaged instead of hanging up.

Will it work with my existing phone system?

Yes — it integrates with all major Australian phone systems including Telstra, Optus and VoIP services. Setup takes about 15 minutes using call forwarding, with no new equipment needed.

How natural does the AI voice sound?

It's trained on Australian accents and business terminology. Most callers can't tell they're speaking with an AI.

Is my data secure and compliant?

Data is stored in Australian data centres and conversations are encrypted, in line with Australian Privacy Principles.

What if the AI can't answer a question?

It's trained on your business information and common industry questions. If it hits something new, it politely gathers the caller's details and lets them know you'll call back with the specific information they need.

How quickly can I get started?

You can be up and running in about 15 minutes — sign up, customise your greeting and business details, then forward your calls. We provide setup guidance and onboarding support.
