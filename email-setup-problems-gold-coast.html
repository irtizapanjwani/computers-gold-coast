Frequently Asked Questions

Email Setup Questions, Answered.

Why is my email not working?

Email issues can happen due to incorrect settings (SMTP, IMAP, POP3), password changes or forgotten login details, email server downtime, ISP restrictions, or corrupt email files.

How much does email setup & repair cost?

Pricing depends on whether it's a new setup vs. troubleshooting an existing issue, the number of devices involved, and the type of problem. Get in touch for a straight quote.

Can you set up email on my phone & computer?

Yes. We configure email across all devices — Windows, Mac, iPhone and Android.

Do you offer email migration services?

Absolutely. We can move all your emails, contacts and settings to a new provider.

Can you fix Telstra (Bigpond) email problems?

Yes. We resolve Bigpond email issues including login failures, password resets, emails not sending or receiving, and sync problems on mobile and desktop.
