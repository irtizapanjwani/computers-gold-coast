Signs Your Battery Needs Attention

Recognise Any Of These?

  • Rapid battery drain — a full charge lasting a fraction of what it used to.
  • Sudden shutdowns — the laptop dies even though there's charge showing.
  • Overheating or swelling — a safety issue, stop using it and get it checked immediately.
  • Battery warning messages — Windows or macOS flagging the battery condition.
  • Not charging fully — stuck below 100%, or charging unusually slowly.
  • Percentage jumping or dropping suddenly — often a calibration issue or a deteriorating cell.

Frequently Asked Questions

Laptop Battery Questions, Answered.

How can I tell if my battery is faulty or if it's a software issue?

Fast drain, not holding a charge, or unexpected shutdowns often point to a faulty battery, but outdated drivers or incorrect power settings can cause similar symptoms. A battery diagnostic tells you which it is before you spend anything.

What is the average lifespan of a laptop battery?

Most laptop batteries last 2 to 4 years, or roughly 300 to 500 charge cycles, depending on usage patterns, charging habits and environmental conditions.

Is it safe to use third-party batteries?

Reputable third-party batteries that meet your laptop's specifications are safe. Low-quality or counterfeit batteries can pose safety risks or underperform — we only fit batteries that meet or exceed OEM standards.

Can I replace the battery in an ultrabook or slim laptop with a non-removable battery?

Yes, in most cases — it just requires a professional to open the chassis properly. We'll assess feasibility and cost as part of the quote.

What is battery calibration, and should I do it?

Calibration means fully charging then fully discharging the battery to reset the battery management system's readings. It's worth doing if your reported percentage seems inaccurate.

Can a swollen laptop battery be repaired?

No — a swollen battery is a safety hazard and should be replaced immediately. Stop using the laptop and get it checked as soon as possible.

How do I check my laptop's battery health myself?

On Windows, generate a Battery Report via Command Prompt. On macOS, hold the Option key and click the battery icon in the menu bar to see its condition.

What's the cost range for professional battery replacement?

Typically $50–$150, depending on the laptop model and battery type. Get a free quote and we'll confirm the exact price for your model before booking.

How do I dispose of my old laptop battery responsibly?

We recycle every battery we replace to local e-waste standards — you don't need to do anything, and it won't end up in landfill.
