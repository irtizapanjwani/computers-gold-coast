Frequently Asked Questions

3D Printer Setup Questions, Answered.

How much does 3D printer setup and calibration cost?

It depends on the printer and how much work is needed. Get a free quote and we'll confirm the price before booking.

Do I need to buy my own printer and filament first?

Yes — bring your own printer, and it helps to have your preferred filament on hand so we can calibrate settings specifically for it.

Can you fix an existing printer that keeps producing failed prints?

Usually, yes. Most repeat print failures come down to bed levelling, calibration or a worn extruder/nozzle — all things we can diagnose and fix.

Which slicer software do you set up?

Whichever suits your printer and workflow — commonly Cura, PrusaSlicer or Bambu Studio, configured with sensible starting profiles for your machine and filament.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance, or just one-off setup?

Both — a one-off setup to get you started, or periodic calibration check-ups if you want your printer kept in top condition over time.
