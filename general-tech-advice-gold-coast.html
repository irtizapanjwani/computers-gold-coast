Frequently Asked Questions

Common Tech Questions, Answered.

How do I speed up Windows 11 after an update without wiping my files?

Disable startup bloat via Task Manager, run "DISM /RestoreHealth", clear the Windows Update cache, and optimise your SSD with Storage Sense. If sluggishness persists, we remote-clean the registry and update chipset drivers.

Why does my NBN modem keep dropping out every evening?

Peak-hour congestion, outdated firmware, or faulty line filters cause most drop-outs. We log DSL stats, update firmware, and — if needed — escalate to your ISP with evidence to expedite a port reset.

Is public WiFi banking safe in Australia?

Only if you use a trusted VPN and enable two-factor authentication. We show you how to set up SMS or authenticator-app 2FA and demonstrate bank-grade VPN settings.

Can you recover deleted files without special software on macOS?

If files were in iCloud Drive, use "Recently Deleted" within 30 days. Otherwise, a Time Machine restore. For permanent deletes, we run recovery scans onsite or in-lab.

Where can I report scam SMS messages in Australia?

Forward to 7226 (SCAM) per Scamwatch, block the sender, and run a malware scan if you tapped any links. We guide you on resetting affected passwords.

What's the easiest way to sync a NAS with OneDrive or Google Drive?

Most NAS units (Synology, QNAP) have a cloud sync package — choose your cloud destination, enable bi-directional sync, set bandwidth limits and schedule daily snapshots. We can remote-configure and test this for you.

Which free safe online banking tips should seniors follow?

Always log out after banking, enable SMS or app-based codes, keep your browser patched, and never click links claiming to be from the ATO or your bank. We offer short senior cyber-safety coaching sessions.
