Frequently Asked Questions

Smart TV & Streaming Setup Questions, Answered.

Do you install all brands of Smart TVs?

Yes, we install and configure all major brands including Samsung, LG, Sony, Hisense, TCL, Panasonic and more.

Can you wall mount my Smart TV?

Absolutely. We provide professional wall mounting with secure brackets and tidy cable management.

Can you help if my TV is buffering or apps are crashing?

Definitely. We optimise your WiFi, update apps, and adjust settings to ensure smooth streaming.

Do you set up parental controls for kids?

Yes. We can create safe profiles, set PIN codes, and restrict content based on age ratings.

Can you connect my TV to Alexa, Google Assistant or Siri?

Absolutely. We integrate Smart TVs with your preferred voice assistant for hands-free control.

Do you offer multi-room or multi-TV setups?

Yes, we can link multiple TVs in your home or business so streaming is seamless across rooms.

Do you integrate soundbars or home theatre systems?

Yes, we configure all audio systems, including surround sound, for optimal performance and sync.

Do you connect gaming consoles to Smart TVs?

Yes, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo and other gaming systems are fully integrated for gaming and streaming.

Do you support older TVs with streaming devices like Chromecast or Apple TV?

Yes, we can set up streaming devices for older TVs to enable Netflix, Disney+ and other apps.

Can you configure a universal remote?

Yes, we can program one remote to control your TV, soundbar and streaming devices.
