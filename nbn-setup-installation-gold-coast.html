Understanding NBN Connection Types

Which One Do You Have?

Different properties are connected to the NBN in different ways. We identify your technology type and configure your setup accordingly.

📺

HFC

Hybrid Fibre Coaxial. Used where Pay TV cables exist. Provides high speeds suitable for streaming and gaming.

🛣️

FTTC

Fibre to the Curb. Fibre runs to the street, with copper to your house. A strong middle-ground for speed and reliability.

📡

FWS

Fixed Wireless & Satellite. For rural or remote areas. We assist in optimising equipment and positioning antennas for best signal.

🌐

FTTP

Fibre to the Premises. Premium connection — full fibre all the way to your home or business, best for high-speed internet and large uploads.

🔌

FTTN

Fibre to the Node. Common in older suburbs. Uses copper from the node to your property; speed depends on distance and line quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

NBN Setup & Installation Questions, Answered.

What is the National Broadband Network (NBN)?

The NBN is Australia's high-speed broadband infrastructure, replacing older networks like ADSL and cable. It improves internet speed, stability, and reliability for homes and businesses.

How do I find out which NBN connection type I have?

You can check your address on the NBN Co website. We also verify your connection type — such as FTTP, FTTN, FTTC, or HFC — when we arrive onsite.

Can you help me choose the right NBN speed plan for my needs?

Yes, we evaluate your usage (streaming, gaming, business apps) and recommend the best speed tier — such as NBN 25, 50, or 100 — for your household or workplace.

Is it better to use WiFi or Ethernet for my NBN setup?

Ethernet offers faster and more stable speeds, especially for gamers and office PCs. We help configure wired and wireless networks to balance speed and convenience.

What brands of NBN modems do you work with?

We install and configure modems from TP-Link, Netgear, D-Link, ASUS, and telco-provided devices from Telstra, Optus, TPG, and Aussie Broadband.

Can you install mesh WiFi systems for large homes?

Yes. We specialise in mesh WiFi installations using systems like Google Nest WiFi, TP-Link Deco, and Netgear Orbi to eliminate dead zones and boost coverage.

Do you configure guest networks for visitors?

Absolutely. We can set up a secure guest network on your NBN WiFi to protect your main devices while offering easy access for clients or family.

Will I need a splitter or filter if I'm switching from ADSL?

No. NBN doesn't require ADSL-style splitters or filters. We ensure old hardware is removed and your setup is fully compatible with fibre or coaxial connections.

Can you help me set up VoIP phones with NBN?

Yes — we integrate VoIP phones into your NBN modem/router or business PBX system and test call quality for work-from-home setups.

What if my modem isn't compatible with NBN?

We assess modem compatibility and recommend or supply a new model that works with your connection type and speed tier.

Is your NBN setup service compatible with smart home devices?

Yes. We help connect your smart TVs, security cameras, Alexa/Google Home devices, and other IoT tech to your new NBN network.

Can you troubleshoot slow NBN speeds or dropouts?

Yes. We perform onsite diagnostics, including speed tests, signal strength checks, and interference scans to fix or report issues.

Do you set up static IP addresses for business use?

Yes, we configure static IPs, dynamic DNS, and port forwarding for remote access, servers, and CCTV setups.

What does a full NBN installation visit include?

A standard visit includes modem setup, WiFi configuration, device connectivity, speed testing, security checks, and user training.

Can you help with modem placement for better coverage?

Absolutely. We analyse signal paths, interference sources, and home layout to place your modem in the ideal location.

Do you support new connections and NBN migrations?

Yes. Whether it's your first NBN connection or a switch from another provider, we handle all the setup steps and ensure minimal downtime.

How do you protect my WiFi network from unauthorised access?

We configure WPA3/WPA2 encryption, strong passwords, and router-level firewall rules to protect your network from threats.

Can you extend WiFi into outdoor areas like patios or sheds?

Yes — we install weatherproof range extenders and access points for outdoor connectivity, ideal for workshops or backyard offices.

Will my old landline still work with NBN?

If you have a VoIP-compatible modem, your landline can still work via the NBN. We'll set up and test it for you during installation.

Can you set up parental controls and content filters?

Yes — we configure built-in parental control tools and, if needed, install third-party DNS-based filters like CleanBrowsing or OpenDNS.
