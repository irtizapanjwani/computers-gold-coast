Frequently Asked Questions

Hardware Installation Questions, Answered.

What makes your hardware installation service different from others on the Gold Coast?

We don't just install hardware — we configure and optimise it. We ensure BIOS/UEFI settings are updated, power delivery is sufficient, and the system is fully benchmarked and tested. We also offer ongoing remote support to tweak settings post-installation.

Can you help if I've bought parts online but don't know how to install them?

Absolutely. Many clients purchase components from online retailers but need help putting them together. We can install them, check compatibility, and let you know before you unbox anything if a part isn't right for your build.

Do you work with both Windows and Mac hardware?

Yes — we install SSDs, RAM and logic board components for both Windows PCs and Apple Macs, including MacBooks and iMacs, and handle OS reinstalls and driver setup after hardware installation. See our Apple Mac repairs page for more.

Can you build a gaming PC from scratch if I give you a budget?

Definitely — tell us your budget and preferences (1440p, 4K gaming, streaming, VR), and we'll select compatible, reliable parts, then build, configure and test everything for peak performance. See our custom computer builds service.

Is remote installation or support really possible for hardware?

We can't physically install components remotely, but we can assist post-install: driver updates, firmware flashing, RAID setup, BIOS tuning and thermal monitoring can all be done remotely once the physical parts are in.

Will you help me understand what you're installing and why?

Of course. Part of our value is education — we'll explain why a particular NVMe SSD is better than a standard SATA SSD, or why you might need a higher-wattage PSU for future-proofing.
