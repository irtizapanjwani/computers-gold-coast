Frequently Asked Questions

Password Manager & 2FA Questions, Answered.

What exactly is a password manager, and why do I need one?

It's a secure vault that stores and auto-fills your passwords, so you only need to remember one master password instead of dozens of different ones. It also makes it practical to use a genuinely strong, unique password for every account.

Isn't my browser's "remember password" feature enough?

It's better than nothing, but it's generally less secure and less portable than a dedicated password manager, and doesn't offer the same account health checks or cross-platform syncing.

What's two-factor authentication, and why bother setting it up?

2FA adds a second step to logging in — usually a code from an app on your phone — so that even if someone gets your password, they still can't get into your account without that second step.

Which accounts should get 2FA first?

Your email is the priority, since it's usually the key to resetting passwords on everything else. Banking, superannuation and social media accounts come next.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Most reputable password managers have a recovery process, but it depends on the account settings you choose during setup. We walk you through this clearly so you're never locked out with no way back in.

Can you set this up across all of my devices?

Yes — computer, phone and tablet, all syncing to the same secure vault.

Is training actually included, or is it just an install?

Training is included. We walk you through using it day-to-day so it actually gets used, rather than being set up once and forgotten.
